OAuth tokens have become critical infrastructure for SaaS integrations, but attackers now exploit them through token theft and session hijacking that mimics legitimate traffic. The post recommends discovery tools, behavioral analytics, and token rotation policies to prevent OAuth abuse and supply chain compromise.
